Common questions

Is my data stored securely?
Yes. Encrypted at rest and in transit, stored in Supabase (EU region). We never sell or share your data.
Does it connect to my bank directly?
Not yet — you import via CSV (supported by all major SA banks). Direct bank feeds are on our roadmap.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes, from settings in one click. Your data export is always available, even after cancelling.
Are tax brackets kept up to date?
Yes. We update brackets, rebates, and medical credits with each SARS budget announcement.
